# Third-party software

Dependencies are downloaded from their upstream projects at configure time.

- [Dear ImGui v1.92.9b](https://github.com/ocornut/imgui/tree/v1.92.9b), MIT
  License.
- [zlib v1.3.2](https://github.com/madler/zlib/tree/v1.3.2), zlib License.

Their license files are available in the corresponding CMake dependency source
directories under `build/_deps/` after configuration. Binary distributions of
`pak-man` should include copies of those licenses.
